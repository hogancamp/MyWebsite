%The subject of annular link homology concerns the categorification of skein modules of the solid torus S^1 x D^2.  Decategorified, such skein modules have many interesting algebraic structures (e.g. operators corresponding to the Dehn twist along a meridian disk, or insertion of a link in a neighborhood the boundary S^1 x S^1 x I) and it would be desireable for the categorification to have analogues of these structures as well.  At present there are many annular link homology theories, but so far none appearing in literature admit categorical analogues of the Dehn twist or action of the skein of the torus.  The issue is that the current annular technology is not ``sufficiently derived’’.  In this talk I will discuss a proposed solution to this problem, which is the notion of derived horizontal trace introduced in joint work with Eugene Gorsky and Paul Wedrich.  For concretenesss I will explain all the constructions in the context of Khovanov homology.
